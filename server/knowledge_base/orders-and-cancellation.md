title: Orders, Cancellation and Changes
category: orders
---

## Can I cancel my order?

Yes, free of charge, any time before the order reaches Shipped. Ask the support
agent to cancel it and the refund is initiated immediately.

Once the order is Shipped, cancellation is no longer possible in the system. Your
options are to refuse the delivery when the courier arrives, or accept it and
raise a return. Refusing is faster because it skips the pickup step.

## Can I cancel just one item from an order?

Yes, as long as that item has not shipped. Items in an order ship independently,
so one item being dispatched does not lock the others. If cancelling an item
breaks a bundle or combo discount, the remaining items are repriced at their
individual rates.

## Why was my order cancelled automatically?

The usual reasons:

- Payment authorisation failed or timed out at the bank
- The item went out of stock between ordering and picking
- The delivery pin code turned out to be non-serviceable
- Three failed delivery attempts
- Address or contact details could not be verified on a high-value order
- Our fraud checks flagged the order for review

In every case the full amount is refunded automatically. You do not need to ask.
If money was debited and the order does not appear in your account at all, the
bank authorisation was never captured and the amount auto-reverses within 5 to 7
business days.

## Can I change the item, size or colour after ordering?

No. Orders cannot be edited after placement. Cancel and reorder if the order has
not shipped. If it has shipped, accept it and use the exchange option, which
supports a different size, colour or variant of the same product.

## Can I add an item to an existing order?

No. Place a separate order. If this pushes either order below the free delivery
threshold, mention it to the agent and a one-time delivery fee waiver can be
applied.

## I was charged but there is no order. What happened?

The payment was authorised but not captured, which happens when the bank
confirms after our checkout session expired. No order exists, so there is nothing
to cancel. The hold on your account releases automatically within 5 to 7
business days.

Give the agent the amount, the date and the last four digits of the card or the
UPI reference, and they can confirm whether any capture reached us.

## I was charged twice for one order.

Duplicate authorisations happen when checkout is retried. Only one is captured;
the other is a hold that drops off within 5 to 7 business days. If both still
show as debited after 7 business days, ask the agent to raise a payment
reconciliation with the reference numbers, which is resolved within 5 business
days.

## Where is my invoice?

The tax invoice is emailed when the order ships and is available under Orders in
the app. Ask the agent to resend it to your registered email if it did not
arrive. GST invoices with a business GSTIN must have the GSTIN entered at
checkout; it cannot be added afterwards.

## Can I order on behalf of someone else?

Yes. Enter their name, address and mobile number as the delivery details. The
delivery OTP goes to the mobile number on the order, so use the recipient's
number, not yours. Warranty stays valid for whoever holds the product.

## How long are orders held for stock?

If an item goes out of stock after you order, we hold the order for 3 business
days while we try to source it. You are notified and can wait or cancel. After 3
days the item is cancelled and refunded automatically.
