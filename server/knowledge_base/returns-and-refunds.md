## How long do I have to return an item?

Most items can be returned within 10 days of delivery. Large appliances
(refrigerators, washing machines, air conditioners, televisions above 43 inches)
have a 7 day return window because they require a technician visit to uninstall.

The window is counted from the delivery date shown on the order, not the order
date. If delivery was delayed, the extra days do not count against you.

## What condition does the item need to be in?

The item must be in its original packaging with all accessories, manuals, free
gifts and the invoice. The serial number sticker must be intact and unpeeled.

We accept returns on opened boxes. Trying the product is not a reason for
rejection. What we cannot accept is physical damage caused after delivery, a
missing serial number sticker, or a device that has been rooted or jailbroken.

## Which items cannot be returned?

- Software, digital downloads and activated licence keys
- Prepaid recharge vouchers and gift cards
- Earphones, in-ear headsets and grooming appliances once the hygiene seal is
  broken
- Items marked "Clearance - no return" on the product page
- Screen protectors and tempered glass once applied

A defective unit in any of these categories is still covered by warranty even
though it cannot be returned for a refund.

## How do I start a return?

Ask the support agent to raise a return request, or do it yourself from Orders
in the app. You will be given a return reference starting with RET. For small
items a courier pickup is scheduled within 2 business days. For large
appliances a technician visits first to verify and uninstall the unit.

You do not need to print anything. The pickup agent carries the label.

## When do I get my money back?

The refund is initiated once the item reaches our warehouse and clears
inspection, which normally takes 2 business days after pickup. After that:

- UPI and wallet: within 24 hours
- Credit or debit card: 5 to 7 business days, back to the same card
- Net banking: 3 to 5 business days
- Cash on delivery: 3 to 5 business days to the bank account you provide
- TechMart Credit: instant

The bank's own processing time is outside our control, so the money can take an
extra cycle to appear on a card statement.

## Do I get the delivery charge back?

Yes, if the return is because the item was defective, damaged, or not what was
ordered. If you are returning because you changed your mind, the original
delivery charge is not refunded and a reverse pickup fee of Rs. 99 applies to
items under Rs. 5,000.

## Can I exchange instead of refunding?

Yes, for a different size, colour or variant of the same product, subject to
stock. Exchanges skip the refund wait entirely: the replacement ships as soon
as the returned unit is picked up. Exchanging for a different product is not
supported, so return and reorder instead.

## What if only part of my order is wrong?

Returns are per item, not per order. Keep what you want and return the rest.
The refund is calculated on the returned items only. If a bundle discount
applied and returning an item breaks the bundle, the discount is recalculated
and the difference is deducted from the refund.
