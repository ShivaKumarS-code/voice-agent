## How long does delivery take?

Estimated delivery is shown at checkout for your pin code and is the reliable
number. Typical ranges:

- Metro cities: 1 to 2 business days
- Tier 2 cities and district headquarters: 2 to 4 business days
- Other serviceable pin codes: 4 to 7 business days
- Large appliances anywhere: 3 to 6 business days, because they move by surface
  freight and need an installation slot

Orders placed before 2 PM on a business day are picked the same day. Orders
after that, or on a Sunday or public holiday, are picked the next business day.

## What does delivery cost?

Free on orders above Rs. 999. Below that, Rs. 59 for standard delivery. Large
appliances are always free regardless of order value.

Same-day delivery is available in selected metro pin codes for Rs. 149 if
ordered before 11 AM. Next-morning delivery is Rs. 99.

## Can I track my order?

Yes. Ask the support agent for the status of your order number and you will get
the current stage, the courier, the tracking number and the expected date. You
can also see it under Orders in the app.

Order stages are: Confirmed, Packed, Shipped, In transit, Out for delivery,
Delivered. A tracking number appears once the order reaches Shipped, which is
usually within 24 hours of ordering.

## My tracking has not updated in two days. Is my order lost?

Usually not. Tracking commonly goes quiet while a shipment moves between
sorting hubs, especially over a weekend or a long surface route. Give it 48
hours past the last scan.

If there has been no scan for 3 business days, or the expected delivery date has
passed, ask the agent to raise a shipment trace. Couriers respond to a trace
within 48 hours. If the shipment is confirmed lost we ship a replacement
immediately at no cost, or refund in full if you prefer.

## Do I have to be home to receive it?

Someone above 18 needs to receive the parcel and share the OTP sent to the
registered mobile number. You can tell the courier to hand it to a family member
or a neighbour, but the OTP still has to be shared with the delivery agent.

For large appliances the person receiving it should be able to point out where
the unit will be installed, since the delivery and installation teams coordinate
on the spot.

## Can I change the delivery address after ordering?

Only before the order is Packed, and only within the same city and pin code
serviceability. Ask the agent to update it. Once the order is Shipped the
address is locked by the courier and cannot be changed; you would need to refuse
delivery and reorder.

## Can I reschedule the delivery date?

Yes. Once the order is Shipped, delivery can be deferred by up to 5 days. Ask
the agent, or respond to the courier's SMS. Large appliance deliveries are tied
to an installation slot, so rescheduling moves both together.

## What if I miss the delivery?

The courier reattempts on the next business day, up to 3 attempts. After 3
failed attempts the parcel returns to us and the order is auto-refunded within 5
business days. To avoid that, reschedule instead of letting attempts lapse.

## What if the box arrives damaged?

Do not accept it. Refuse the delivery and tell the agent on the call; a
replacement is dispatched at no cost and no return pickup is needed. If you have
already accepted it and then find damage, report it within 48 hours with photos
of the box and the item, and it is treated as a damaged-in-transit claim with a
free replacement.

## Is installation included?

Yes, and it is free for televisions, air conditioners, washing machines,
refrigerators, dishwashers, chimneys and geysers. The installation team calls
within 24 to 48 hours of delivery to fix a slot.

Installation is a separate visit from delivery. Do not unbox and self-install an
air conditioner or a washing machine; doing so can void the warranty on the
sealed system.

Mounting hardware, wall brackets, copper piping beyond the standard 3 metres,
stabilisers and electrical work are chargeable extras quoted by the technician
on site.

## Do you deliver outside India?

No. TechMart delivers only within India, and a small number of remote pin codes
are not serviceable. Enter the pin code on the product page to check before
ordering.
