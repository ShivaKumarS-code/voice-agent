## What payment methods do you accept?

- UPI: Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM and any UPI app
- Credit and debit cards: Visa, Mastercard, RuPay, American Express
- Net banking from all major banks
- Wallets: Paytm, Amazon Pay, Mobikwik
- EMI: credit card EMI, debit card EMI on selected banks, and cardless EMI
- TechMart Credit, our store credit line
- Cash on delivery, on orders up to Rs. 50,000 in serviceable pin codes

## Is cash on delivery available?

Yes on orders up to Rs. 50,000, in most but not all pin codes. It is not
available on large appliances, on clearance items, or where our checks flag the
address. A Rs. 40 handling fee applies to cash on delivery orders below
Rs. 999.

The delivery agent accepts cash, UPI and card at the door. You cannot open the
box before paying, but you can refuse the delivery if the box looks damaged.

## What EMI options are there?

Tenures of 3, 6, 9, 12, 18 and 24 months, on orders above Rs. 3,000.

No-cost EMI is available on selected products at 3, 6 and 9 months. On a no-cost
EMI the interest the bank charges is offset by an equivalent discount from us, so
the total you pay matches the product price. The bank may still show interest on
your statement; the discount was already applied to the price at checkout.

Beyond the no-cost tenures, standard bank interest applies, typically 13 to 16
percent annually. The exact per-month amount is shown at checkout before you
confirm.

## Can I get EMI without a credit card?

Yes, two ways. Debit card EMI is offered by several banks including HDFC, ICICI,
Axis, Kotak and SBI if you have a pre-approved limit; eligibility is checked
instantly at checkout using your card number. Cardless EMI through our finance
partners needs a one-time KYC and gives you a reusable limit.

## Can I convert a completed purchase to EMI?

Not through TechMart, but most banks let you convert any transaction above a
threshold into EMI from their own app or by calling them, usually within 30 days
of the transaction. The terms are then set by your bank, not by us.

## Are there processing fees on EMI?

Banks charge a one-time processing fee on some EMI plans, generally Rs. 199 to
Rs. 499, and it appears on your card statement, not on our invoice. Cardless EMI
partners may charge a similar fee. The amount is disclosed at checkout before you
confirm.

## What happens to my EMI if I return the item?

The refund goes back to the card and cancels the remaining instalments. Interest
and processing fees already charged by the bank for completed cycles are usually
not reversed by the bank, and a no-cost EMI discount is reversed proportionally.
Expect one or two more statement cycles before the EMI closes.

## How do bank offers and instant discounts work?

Instant discounts are applied at checkout when you pay with the qualifying bank
card, and you see the reduced amount before confirming. Cashback offers are paid
by the bank to your account later, typically within 90 days, and we cannot track
or expedite those.

One bank offer per order. Bank offers do not stack with coupon codes unless the
coupon explicitly says so. If a return breaks the minimum order value the offer
required, the discount is deducted from the refund.

## My coupon code is not working.

The common causes, in order of likelihood: the cart is below the minimum order
value, the code is limited to first-time customers, the item's category or brand
is excluded, the code has expired, or you have already used a single-use code.
The checkout page states which condition failed. If the code was issued to you
by TechMart support and still fails, the agent can apply it manually.

## Is it safe to pay on TechMart?

Card details are not stored on our servers. Payments run through PCI-DSS
compliant gateways and every transaction needs OTP or 3D Secure authentication.

TechMart staff will never ask for your full card number, CVV, UPI PIN, OTP, or
account password, on a call or otherwise. Nobody from TechMart will ask you to
install a screen-sharing app or approve a payment request to receive a refund.
Refunds always go back to the original payment method automatically and never
require you to pay or authorise anything. If a caller claims otherwise, hang up
and report it.
