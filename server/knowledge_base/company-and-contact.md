## What is TechMart?

TechMart is an electronics retailer selling mobile phones, laptops, televisions,
home appliances, audio and accessories, online and through physical stores across
India. We have been operating since 2016.

## What are your support hours?

Phone and chat support run every day, 8 AM to 10 PM IST, including Sundays. The
automated voice agent answers instantly at any hour; requests that need a human
outside those hours are queued and called back the next morning.

Service appointments, repairs and technician visits run Monday to Saturday,
10 AM to 7 PM.

## How do I reach a human agent?

Say so on the call and you will be transferred, or ask for a callback if the wait
is long. Callbacks are offered within the support window and you keep your place
in the queue.

Wait times are typically under 3 minutes on weekday afternoons and longest on
Monday mornings and during sale periods.

## What are your store hours?

Retail stores are open every day, 11 AM to 9 PM, including Sundays and most
public holidays. Individual stores in malls follow the mall's timings, so check
the store page for your city.

## Can I buy online and collect in store?

Yes, in cities with a TechMart store. Choose Store Pickup at checkout. The order
is ready in 4 hours for in-stock items and you get an SMS when it is. Bring the
order number and a photo ID.

Unclaimed pickups are held for 7 days, then cancelled and refunded.

## Can I return an online order to a store?

Yes, for small items, within the normal return window and with the invoice and
original packaging. Store returns skip the courier pickup, so the refund starts
the same day. Large appliances cannot be returned to a store; those need a
technician visit to uninstall.

## Do stores price-match the website?

Yes. If the website price is lower on the same day, the store honours it. Ask at
the counter before billing. Store-exclusive and online-exclusive offers do not
cross over.

## Do you buy old devices?

Yes, exchange is available on phones, laptops and televisions. The quoted value
depends on model, age and condition, and is deducted from the new purchase price
at checkout. The old device is collected at delivery, and the pickup agent
verifies its condition; if it does not match what you declared, the quote is
revised on the spot and you can decline the exchange.

## How do I raise a complaint?

Tell the agent you want to file a complaint and they will log it with a reference
starting with CMP. You get email updates. First response is within 24 hours and
resolution targets 5 business days.

If you are not satisfied, ask for escalation to a supervisor, and after that to
the grievance officer, whose details are published in the Contact section of the
website as required by consumer protection rules.

## Is my personal data safe?

We collect only what is needed to fulfil orders and provide support, and we do
not sell personal data. Calls with the voice agent are recorded and transcribed
for quality and training, and you can ask the agent to not record, or to delete
a past recording.

You can request a copy of your data or the deletion of your account by asking
the agent to raise the request; it is actioned within 30 days.

## Does TechMart ever call me asking for payment details?

No. Nobody from TechMart will ask for your card number, CVV, UPI PIN, OTP or
password, and we never ask you to install a screen-sharing or remote-access app.
Refunds are automatic to the original payment method and never require you to
pay, authorise a request, or share a code.

If you get such a call, hang up and report it to support. Fraudsters do
impersonate retailers, especially after a genuine order.
