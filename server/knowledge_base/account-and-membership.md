## How do I create an account?

Sign up with your mobile number and verify the OTP. An email address is optional
but recommended, because invoices and refund confirmations go there. You can
order as a guest, but guest orders are harder to track and cannot use TechMart
Credit or the loyalty programme.

## I cannot log in. What should I check?

Almost always one of: the OTP went to an old mobile number, the SMS was delayed,
or the account was created with a different number than the one you are trying.
OTPs are valid for 10 minutes and you can request a new one after 60 seconds.

After 5 failed attempts the account locks for 30 minutes. Ask the agent to verify
your identity and clear the lock early.

## How do I change my registered mobile number?

Ask the agent. Changing the primary number needs verification against a recent
order, because it controls account access and delivery OTPs. Once verified, the
change takes effect immediately and in-flight deliveries keep using the old
number for that order's OTP.

## What is TechMart Plus?

A paid membership at Rs. 499 per year:

- Free delivery on every order regardless of value
- Early access to sales, 12 hours before general opening
- Extra 5 percent off on accessories
- Priority support, which skips the queue
- Extended return window of 15 days instead of 10
- Two free technician visits per year outside warranty

It pays for itself at roughly eight small orders a year. Cancel any time within
the first 30 days for a full refund if you have not used a member benefit;
after that it runs to the end of the term.

## What is TechMart Credit?

A store credit line for eligible customers, with a limit between Rs. 10,000 and
Rs. 2,00,000 set after a one-time KYC and credit check. It is used at checkout
like a payment method and supports no-cost EMI on more products than card EMI.

Refunds to TechMart Credit are instant, which is the fastest refund route we
have.

## How do loyalty points work?

You earn 1 point per Rs. 100 spent, and 100 points equal Rs. 10 off. Points post
after the return window closes, not at delivery, because a returned order does
not earn. They expire 12 months after they are earned.

Points cannot be redeemed on gift cards or recharges, and at most 20 percent of
an order value can be paid with points.

## Why am I getting so many notifications?

Order updates are transactional and always sent. Marketing messages can be turned
off: ask the agent to unsubscribe you from promotional SMS, email and WhatsApp,
or do it in Settings under Notifications. Transactional messages about your
orders and repairs continue either way, because they carry delivery OTPs.

## How do I delete my account?

Ask the agent to raise a deletion request. It is actioned within 30 days. Open
orders, active repairs, and outstanding TechMart Credit must be settled first.
Deleting the account forfeits unused loyalty points and any remaining Plus
membership, neither of which is refundable at that point.

Invoices are retained for 8 years after deletion because tax law requires it;
everything else is removed.
