## How do I book a service appointment?

Ask the support agent. They need three things: your order number or the product,
a short description of the fault, and your preferred day. The agent checks open
slots and confirms one on the call. You get a booking reference starting with APT
by SMS and email.

Appointments can be booked up to 21 days ahead. Same-day slots are not
bookable through support; for an urgent breakdown ask the agent to flag the
request as urgent and the service team calls back within 4 hours.

## What are the appointment slots?

Service runs Monday to Saturday, 10 AM to 7 PM. Slots are two hours wide:
10-12, 12-2, 2-4, 4-6. Sunday and public holidays are closed for both carry-in
and home visits.

The technician calls 30 minutes before arriving. A slot is a window, not an exact
time, because travel between visits varies.

## Home visit or carry-in?

It depends on the product, and the agent will tell you which applies:

- Home visit: refrigerators, washing machines, air conditioners, dishwashers,
  televisions above 43 inches, chimneys, geysers
- Carry-in: mobile phones, tablets, laptops, cameras, headphones, small kitchen
  appliances, televisions 43 inches and below

If no service centre is within 25 km of your pin code, carry-in items qualify for
a free courier pickup and return instead.

## Where are your service centres?

TechMart operates authorised service centres in Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi NCR,
Hyderabad, Chennai, Pune, Kolkata and Ahmedabad, plus partner centres in around
60 other cities. The agent can give you the nearest address and its phone number
for your pin code.

Mobile phones are serviced at the brand's own authorised centre rather than ours,
because handset repairs need brand-certified parts to keep the warranty intact.

## What should I have ready for the appointment?

- The invoice, digital is fine
- The warranty card, if you have it
- The product with its accessories, for a carry-in
- For a home visit: clear access to the appliance, and someone above 18 present

For a laptop or phone, back up your data first and remove any screen lock or
account lock. Repairs sometimes require a factory reset, and a locked device can
be refused. We are not liable for data loss during repair.

## Can I reschedule or cancel an appointment?

Yes, free of charge up to 4 hours before the slot starts. Ask the agent with your
APT reference. Inside 4 hours, a home visit that has already been dispatched is
billed at the Rs. 350 visit charge.

If nobody is present for a home visit, it counts as a missed appointment and the
visit charge applies to the rebooking.

## How long does a repair take?

The technician diagnoses on the first visit. Simple faults are fixed on the spot.
If a part is needed, expect 5 to 7 business days for the part to arrive and a
second visit to be scheduled. Imported parts can take up to 3 weeks, and you are
told the estimate before work begins.

Carry-in repairs average 5 to 7 business days from drop-off. You can ask the
agent for the status using your APT reference at any point.

## Do I get a standby unit while mine is repaired?

Not routinely. For refrigerators and washing machines out of action for more than
10 days under warranty, ask the agent to request a standby unit; approval depends
on local availability.

## Is there a charge for a diagnosis?

Not during warranty. Outside warranty, a home visit diagnosis is Rs. 350 and a
carry-in diagnosis is free. The Rs. 350 is waived if you approve the repair
quote. You always see the quote before any work starts, and you can decline.
