## What warranty comes with my purchase?

Every product sold by TechMart carries the manufacturer's standard warranty,
which is 1 year for most electronics and 2 years for large appliances. Some
categories differ:

- Mobile phones: 1 year on the device, 6 months on the in-box charger and cable
- Laptops: 1 year, extendable to 3 years at purchase
- Televisions: 1 year comprehensive plus 1 year on the panel
- Refrigerators and washing machines: 2 years comprehensive plus 5 to 10 years
  on the compressor or motor depending on the model
- Air conditioners: 1 year comprehensive plus 5 years on the compressor
- Accessories such as cases, cables and bags: 6 months

The exact terms are printed on the warranty card in the box and shown on the
product page under Warranty.

## What does the warranty not cover?

- Physical damage, cracked screens and dents
- Liquid damage, unless the model is rated for water resistance and the damage
  falls within that rating
- Damage from voltage fluctuation where no stabiliser was used, on appliances
  that require one
- Normal wear such as battery capacity decline below 80 percent after a year of
  use, scratches and fading
- Unauthorised repair, opened casing, or a broken tamper seal
- Consumables: filters, bags, belts, brush heads, printer cartridges
- Software problems, virus infection and data loss

## Do I need the invoice to claim warranty?

Yes. The invoice is the proof of purchase date. You do not need a paper copy;
the support agent can pull the invoice from your order history and the service
centre accepts the digital copy from the app. Keep the warranty card if you have
it, but the invoice alone is usually enough.

## Is my warranty valid if I bought the item as a gift?

Yes. Warranty follows the product, not the buyer. The service centre will accept
the original invoice regardless of whose name is on it.

## How do I claim warranty service?

Ask the support agent to book a service appointment. You will be asked for the
order number and a short description of the fault. What happens next depends on
the product:

- Small portable items: carry-in to the nearest service centre, or a free
  courier pickup if no centre is within 25 km
- Large appliances and televisions: a technician visits your address
- Mobile phones: carry-in to an authorised brand service centre

Turnaround is 5 to 7 business days once the unit is received. Parts that have to
be imported can extend this to 3 weeks, and you will be told before work starts.

## Is warranty repair free?

Labour and covered parts are free during the warranty period. A technician home
visit for a large appliance is also free. If the inspection finds the fault is
not covered, you are told the repair estimate first and can decline. Declining
after a home visit incurs a Rs. 350 visit charge.

## What if the product fails again after repair?

Repairs carry a 90 day guarantee on the same fault. If the same problem returns
within 90 days the follow-up repair is free even if the original warranty has
since expired. Three failed repairs for the same fault within the warranty
period qualify the unit for replacement; ask the agent to escalate for a
replacement review.

## Can I buy extended warranty?

Yes. TechMart Protect can be added at purchase or within 30 days of delivery on
items above Rs. 10,000. It extends comprehensive cover by 1 or 2 years and can
include accidental damage protection for screens. Pricing is roughly 4 to 8
percent of the product price per year. It is transferable if you sell the item
and refundable pro-rata if you cancel within the first year.

## The product is defective right out of the box. What now?

That is handled as a return, not a warranty repair, as long as you report it
within the return window. You get a replacement or a full refund including
delivery charges, and there is no repair wait. Report it as soon as you notice,
and keep the packaging.
